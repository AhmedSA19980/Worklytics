# Worklytics

**Worklytics** is a Workforce Performance Intelligence Platform designed to help organizations monitor, analyze, and improve employee performance through centralized data, configurable KPIs, system integrations, and AI-powered insights.

The platform connects employee and business activity data from different systems and transforms it into meaningful performance metrics. Managers can track individual and team performance, identify trends, detect potential performance issues, and receive actionable recommendations to support better decision-making.

## Key Features

* Employee, department, position, and organization management
* Role-based access control and permissions
* Configurable performance KPIs based on employee positions
* Employee and team performance monitoring
* Performance scoring and trend analysis
* Automated alerts for unusual performance changes
* Integration with external business systems
* Centralized activity and performance data
* AI-powered performance analysis and recommendations
* Manager action plans and performance improvement tracking
* Analytics and reporting dashboards

## Project Vision


Worklytics aims to solve a common business challenge: organizations often collect large amounts of employee and operational data across different systems, but turning that data into actionable insights can be difficult.


Worklytics provides a centralized platform where organizations can:

> **Collect data → Measure performance → Detect problems → Analyze patterns → Take action → Measure improvement**

The goal is not to replace managerial decision-making, but to provide managers with meaningful insights and recommendations that help them identify potential issues and make more informed decisions.

## Example Use Case

A sales employee's performance decreases significantly over several months.


Worklytics can detect the decline through configured KPIs and analyze related data such as sales volume, attendance, customer ratings, and historical performance trends.

The system can then provide insights such as:

* Performance decreased by 20% over the last two months.
* Attendance remained stable.
* Customer satisfaction remained consistent.
* Sales transaction volume decreased significantly.

Based on this information, the platform can suggest possible actions for managers to investigate, such as reviewing sales assignments, comparing team performance, or creating a performance improvement plan.

## Architecture Concept


Worklytics is designed around the following workflow:

```text
External Business Systems
        ↓
Integration Layer
        ↓
Data Normalization
        ↓
Performance & KPI Engine
        ↓
Trend and Anomaly Detection
        ↓
AI-Powered Insights
        ↓
Manager Recommendations
        ↓
Action Plans
        ↓
Performance Improvement Tracking
```
Worklytics is  being developed as a modern full-stack application with a focus on scalable architecture, real-world business problems, and data-driven decision-making.
